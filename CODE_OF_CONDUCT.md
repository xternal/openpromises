# Code of conduct

OpenPromises is used by people who disagree about politics. The project works only if everyone can take part whatever their views, so the way we treat each other here follows the same rule as the cards: one standard for everyone.

## What we expect

- **Argue about the work, not the person.** Disagree with a rule, a change or a finding, with reasons and evidence. Never attack someone for who they are, or for whom they vote.
- **Leave party politics at the door.** Issues and pull requests are about how the engine works, never about which party is right. A change is judged by whether it keeps the same standard for every party (CONTRIBUTING.md, ground rule 3).
- **Be patient with newcomers** and with people writing in a second language. Plain words help everyone.
- **Keep people safe.** Never post someone's private details, and never put anyone at risk, least of all people reporting from countries where it is dangerous to do so.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, threats, insults, slurs, sexual language or imagery, deliberate intimidation, publishing someone's private information, and repeatedly derailing a discussion.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code privately to the maintainer, Pavel Guzhikov ([github.com/xternal](https://github.com/xternal)). If you have no other way to reach him, open an issue that says only that you need to talk privately. Every report is read, and the reporter's identity stays private.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions, and block anyone who breaks this code, here and in the project's other spaces. Decisions are explained to the people involved.
